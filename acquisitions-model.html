Forward Looking Model - Acquisition Screener

Purpose-Built Student Housing · Rankings & Key Metrics

Columns marked FCST reflect forecasted values, not trailing actuals
Fwd. Rank
Forward-looking market rank based on a forecasted composite score of demand, supply, and occupancy signals. Lower = more attractive.
Curr. Rank
Current-year ranking based on the existing model. Used as a baseline for measuring directional change.
Change
Difference between current and forward-looking rank (Current − Forward). Positive = market improved; negative = market declined.
FT Enrollment
Full-time student enrollment at the associated university. A proxy for market size and base demand for off-campus housing.
TTM Prelease
Trailing 12-month prelease rate for purpose-built student housing. Higher prelease indicates stronger near-term demand.
PBSH Occ.
Purpose-Built Student Housing occupancy rate. Measures how fully occupied existing PBSH stock is in the market.
3yr Bed/Enroll Δ  Fcst
Forecast. Projected 3-year change in the student-bed-to-enrollment ratio. Rising ratio signals new supply outpacing enrollment growth.
TTM Prelease Δ
Year-over-year change in trailing 12-month prelease rate. Positive means leasing momentum is accelerating.
3yr App. Growth  Fcst
Forecast. Projected 3-year change in university applications. Indicates long-run demand trajectory and enrollment pipeline strength.
POSH Occ. LY
Private Off-Campus Student Housing occupancy from last year. Captures the broader off-campus rental market health.
FT OoS UG Growth
Growth in full-time out-of-state undergraduate enrollment. OoS students are more likely to rent, boosting PBSH demand.
Strongest / Weakest
The single variable with the highest or lowest normalized score for that market - flags what is driving or limiting the ranking.
