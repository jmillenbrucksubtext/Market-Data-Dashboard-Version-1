Market KPIs (top of market detail page)

Nine tiles that summarise a market's current snapshot.

Existing Beds
Count of purpose-built student-housing beds in the market at the latest snapshot. scorecard.existing_beds = sum of dbo.MarketReports.beds_purpose_built on the latest snapshot_date per market.
Pipeline Beds
Lease-up + under-construction + planned beds. scorecard.beds_pipeline_total = beds_lease_up + beds_under_construction + beds_planned from MarketReports.
Penetration
Purpose-built supply relative to anchor enrollment. penetration_ratio = existing_beds / enr_full_time. Higher = more PBSH supply per student.
FTE Enrollment
Full-time-equivalent enrollment at the anchor university. scorecard.enr_full_time ← dbo.MarketReports.enr_full_time (sourced from IPEDS, refreshed annually per school).
Avg Rent / Bed
Bed-weighted average effective rent across the market's properties at the latest snapshot. scorecard.avg_rent_per_bed ← dbo.MarketReports.rate_avg.
Rent YoY
Year-over-year change in avg_rent_per_bed from market_history, comparing the latest snapshot to the same calendar day one year prior.
Occupancy
Bed-weighted occupancy at the latest snapshot. scorecard.occupancy ← dbo.MarketReports.occupancy.
Pre-lease
Bed-weighted pre-lease at the latest snapshot. scorecard.prelease ← dbo.MarketReports.prelease.
Mean Origin Income
Average household income of the census tracts incoming students migrated from. Computed in load_affluence.py from monthly Migration CSVs (Affluence Data/MigrationAllRents*.csv) joined to IPEDS → market_key crosswalk. Pooled over two reference months for stability.

Market Performance Charts

Multi-year time-series benchmarked against the Subtext-30 average. Each year is anchored to the same month/day as the latest snapshot so YoY is apples-to-apples.

Market Rent
Line chart of market_history.avg_rent_per_bed over years (anchored). Solid rust = this market; dashed everest = bed-weighted average of all Subtext-30 markets at the same anchor.
Market Rent Growth
YoY % change derived from the same series: (rent_t − rent_{t-1}) / rent_{t-1}. First year shows no value (no prior).
Market Occupancy
market_history.occupancy over years (anchored). Same dual-line treatment.
Market Pre-lease
market_history.prelease over years (anchored).
Penetration Ratio
For each year: existing_beds / enr_full_time at the anchored snapshot.
Bed Supply: Existing + Pipeline
Stacked bars per year: existing, lease-up, under-construction, planned. From market_history bed columns.
Pre-leasing Velocity by Cycle
Lines from prelease_velocity table — one line per leasing cycle, x-axis = week-of-cycle, y-axis = bed-weighted prelease %. Shows how this year's pace stacks against prior cycles week-by-week.

Score formula. For each qualifier, a credit_fraction ∈ [0,1]: binary qualifiers are 0 or 1; rent_growth_3yr awards years_passed / 3. NA qualifiers are excluded. score_pct = sum(credit_fraction) / count(evaluable). Badge tier: ≥ 80% green, 60–80% amber, < 60% red.

Per-row colour. Each scorecard row is strictly pass (green) / fail (red) / NA (grey). The previous “warn” middle band was retired — partial credit lives only in the aggregate, not the row state.

Comp-set Definition

Which properties count as the "comp set" for each market. Computed in load_comp_set.py and stamped onto every property as is_comp_set.

Eligibility pool
phase ∈ {stable, lease up} AND milesToClosestCampus ≤ 1.0
Comp-set members (within pool — union)
(a) Built ≥ 2020 (yearBuilt ≥ 2020), or
(b) Top 5 by avg_rent (rent per bed) within the walkable pool, regardless of vintage.
Why the OR rescue
~112 of 279 markets have zero pool properties built in 2020+. The top-5-by-rent rule guarantees every market with at least one walkable PBSH gets a comp-set, so the rent_compset qualifier never goes NA for that reason alone.
Knobs (top of load_comp_set.py)
COMP_SET_MILES = 1.0 · COMP_SET_VINTAGE = 2020 · COMP_SET_TOPN = 5 · COMP_SET_PHASES = {'stable', 'lease up'}

Comp-set Performance Charts

Multi-year line charts on the Comps tab. Show the bed-weighted aggregate across the comps you have checked vs the broader market.

For each year and metric, the Comps line is the bed-weighted average across selected comps' historical snapshots: Σ(value × beds) / Σ(beds) over rows in property_history for that (property_key, year) pair. The Market line is the same anchored market value from market_history. Toggling checkboxes recomputes the comps line live.

Avg Rent / Bed
Comps: bed-weighted property_history.avg_rent_per_bed. Market: market_history.avg_rent_per_bed.
Rent / SF
Comps: bed-weighted property_history.avg_rent_per_sf. Market: same field from market_history (when populated).
Occupancy
Bed-weighted property_history.occupancy vs market_history.occupancy.
Pre-lease
Bed-weighted property_history.prelease vs market_history.prelease.

Historical anchor. Each year picks the snapshot closest to the latest-snapshot calendar day (e.g., 2026-05-09 vs 2025-05-10 vs 2024-05-11 …). At property level this is aggregated from dbo.PlanReports via load_property_history.py — bed-weighted across plans within each property snapshot.

Industry Tab

The national overview on index.html.

Industry map
One pin per market at the anchor university. Pin colour = qualifier-score tier (≥ 80% green, 60–80% amber, < 60% red, NA grey). Pin size scales with existing_beds. Toggle controls filter to Subtext-30 only or Power-4 conference markets.
Industry scorecard (top-30 table)
Markets ranked by current rent. Columns: rent, beds, FTE, occupancy, prelease, qualifier mini-badge (small percentage pill, same tier scheme as the map). The ★ flag shows whether any of the market's universities is in the Subtext-30 list.
Rent / Occupancy / Pre-lease ladders
Bar charts ranking the Subtext-30 markets on a single metric. Bars coloured by Subtext-30 membership.
