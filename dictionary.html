Student Migration tab

Two separately normalized CBSA heatmaps built from the MigrationOnly source.

School matching

UNIQUEID is matched exactly to campus_locations.ipeds_id. The generator fails on conflicting duplicate rows or ambiguous dashboard matches and writes a complete audit to student_migration/audit.json.

In-state origin heatmap

in-state CBSA MigrantsIn / total in-state MigrantsIn. The full in-state dataset reconciles to 100% independently of the overall in-state/out-of-state mix.

Out-of-state origin heatmap

out-of-state CBSA MigrantsIn / total out-of-state MigrantsIn. This is a second independent 100% distribution with its own heat scale.

Geography and limitations

State determines in-state versus out-of-state. CBSAID is plotted at the U.S. Census Bureau 2021 Gazetteer internal point; heat indicates relative origin share, not a precise household or student address. Codes 99999 and NA remain in the 100% reconciliation but are not assigned guessed map coordinates. The source does not contain a reporting-period field.

Shadow Market tab

How the Shadow Market Estimate Works

The approved CoStar + Census methodology for estimating college-age renters outside large purpose-built properties.

Step 1 - Capture CoStar multifamily inventory

CoStar multifamily properties with 5–49 units are included. Properties typed as student housing, demolished properties, and buildings with 50 or more units are excluded.

approved CoStar inventory = multifamily properties with 5–49 units

Step 2 - Add the small-property gap

Census renter inventory in 2–4-unit structures is added because CoStar does not comprehensively capture those properties. One-unit rentals are intentionally excluded because they materially overstated the population.

combined units = CoStar 5–49 units + Census 2–4 units

Step 3 - Estimate renter population

Each CoStar building is matched to its nearest Census block group. Census population and unit tables provide average occupancy for renter inventory below 50 units. The same local occupancy is applied to CoStar units and Census 2–4-unit inventory.

estimated population = combined units × local sub-50 occupancy

Step 4 - Isolate the college-age renter share

The Census renter share age 15–24 is narrowed to the approved 18–21 age window using local population age distributions. That percentage is applied independently within each block group before results are summed.

shadow population = estimated population × renter 15–24 share × age 18–21 tightening

Geography and distance

Calculations are performed separately for each Census block group. Each block group is represented by its Census centroid and assigned once to the nearest campus distance ring.

How to interpret the heatmap

The heatmap uses the broader Census block-group distribution because it produced the more accurate neighborhood pattern during market validation. The KPI cards and ring table still use the approved CoStar + Census calculation. The map is a distribution aid, not a separate official total or exact student-address map.

Primary Census tables

ACS 5-Year tables B25007 (renter age), B25032 (renter units by structure size), and B25033 (renter population by structure size). B01001 narrows age 15–24 to ages 18–21.

Scope and overlap controls

Each dashboard market is generated once by market key and uses only its anchor university. Repeated campus records are collapsed, CoStar properties are deduplicated by PropertyID or location, and every Census block group is assigned once.

Reference

Shared concepts

Cross-cutting rules and formulas referenced by multiple tabs.

Scoring formula

How the qualifier scorecard rolls up to a single per-market percentage.

Each qualifier has a credit_fraction ∈ [0, 1]:

  • Binary qualifiers (12 of 13): 0 or 1.
  • Multi-component qualifiers with a breakdown list (rent_growth_3yr): passing_components / total_components - e.g. 2 of 3 trailing years ≥ 3% → 0.667 credit.
  • NA qualifiers are excluded entirely.

Per-market score: sum(credit_fraction) / count(evaluable).

Aggregate tier (badge / pin colour): ≥ 80% green · 60–80% amber · < 60% red.

Per-row state (scorecard cells): strictly PASS / FAIL / NA. The previous "warn" middle band was retired.

Comp-set rule

Which properties count as comps in a market - drives the Competitive Set tab and the comp-rent qualifier.

Eligibility pool: phase = stable (stabilized only - lease-up and pipeline excluded) AND milesToClosestCampus ≤ 1.0 AND beds ≥ 150

Comp-set member (within pool - union):

  • (a) Built ≥ 2020 (yearBuilt ≥ 2020), or
  • (b) Top 5 by avg_rent within the pool, regardless of vintage.

The OR rescue: ~112 of 279 markets have zero pool properties built 2020+. Without the top-5 fallback, those markets' rent-comp qualifier would always be NA.

Configurable knobs (top of load_comp_set.py): COMP_SET_MILES = 1.0 · COMP_SET_VINTAGE = 2020 · COMP_SET_TOPN = 5 · COMP_SET_MIN_BEDS = 150 · COMP_SET_PHASES = {stable, lease up}.

Snapshot anchoring

Why every "annual" value lines up to the same calendar day.

The dataset has weekly snapshots, not yearly. For multi-year comparisons we pick one snapshot per (entity, year) - the one whose snapshot_date is closest to the latest snapshot's month/day. So if the latest pull is 2026-05-09, the 2025 anchor is the row closest to 2025-05-09, the 2024 anchor closest to 2024-05-09, etc.

Used in market_history, property_history, and the rent_growth_3yr qualifier. The anchor moves automatically as the weekly refresh advances "latest".
