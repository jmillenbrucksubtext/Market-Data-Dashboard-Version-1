Sources

Every figure on the Industry tab traces back to a column in StudentResearch. Below: the base tables we read, then a row-by-row map from each on-page data point to its exact SQL source. The SQL bodies are in j.dashboard-app/export-data.py.

Base tables read from StudentResearch

The dashboard reads only these tables. Read-only via db_datareader.

TableRoleGrain
dbo.MarketReports Weekly market-level snapshot fact. Drives 7 of 9 metrics. 1 row per market_key × snapshot_date
dbo.Markets Market dimension (city, state, region). 1 row per market (Key)
dbo.Schools_Denormal University dimension with current-year enrollment. 1 row per university (schoolKey)
dbo.Enrollments_Manual Historical enrollment time-series. Drives YoY and 5-yr CAGR. 1 row per IPEDS_ID × Year
dbo.IPEDS_CH_Crosswalk Maps IPEDS_ID → marketKey. 1 row per (school, market)
dbo.Subtext30 The 30 Subtext focus universities (drives the ★ flag). 1 row per focus university

Labels & identifiers
Anchor University (scorecard, charts) dbo.Schools_Denormal.name Largest school by enrollmentTotal per marketKey. Fallback: Markets.city + ', ' + Markets.stateAbbr if no school is mapped.

Supply
Existing Beds dbo.MarketReports.beds_purpose_built At MAX(snapshot_date) per market_key.
Pipeline · Lease-up dbo.MarketReports.beds_lease_up At MAX(snapshot_date). Roughly 0–6 months to delivery.
Pipeline · Under Construction dbo.MarketReports.beds_under_construction At MAX(snapshot_date). Roughly 6–24 months.
Pipeline · Planned dbo.MarketReports.beds_planned At MAX(snapshot_date). Roughly 12–36+ months.
Pipeline Total (scorecard column) dbo.MarketReports.beds_pipeline Source-rolled total of the three stages above.
Demand
Total Enrollment (scorecard) dbo.MarketReports.enr_total Market-level total at MAX(snapshot_date).
Enrollment by University (chart) dbo.Schools_Denormal.enrollmentTotal Current academic year (enrollmentYear).
Enrollment YoY dbo.Enrollments_Manual.Total_Enrollment (current_year / prev_year) − 1, joined on IPEDS_ID.
Enrollment 5-yr CAGR dbo.Enrollments_Manual.Total_Enrollment (current / current−5)^(1/5) − 1. NULL if <5 years of history.
Pricing
Avg Rent / Bed dbo.MarketReports.rate_avg Source-computed bed-weighted average at MAX(snapshot_date).
Rent YoY dbo.MarketReports.rate_avg (current / prior) − 1 where prior = snapshot closest to current_date − 365d (within ±30 days).
Bed weight (rent reliability) dbo.MarketReports.beds_tracked_by_occupancy Number of beds the rent average is computed from.
Penetration & band
Penetration Ratio dbo.MarketReports.beds_purpose_built / dbo.MarketReports.enr_total At MAX(snapshot_date) per market. NULL if enrollment = 0.
Penetration Band derived < 0.30 Under-supplied · 0.30–0.55 Balanced · > 0.55 Over-supplied.
Pre-leasing velocity
Pre-lease % dbo.MarketReports.prelease AVG(prelease) grouped by market_key × leasing_cycle × week_of_cycle. Limited to last 3 cycles and top 50 markets by tracked beds.
Leasing cycle derived from MarketReports.snapshot_date CASE WHEN MONTH(snapshot_date) ≤ 8 THEN YEAR(snapshot_date) ELSE YEAR(snapshot_date)+1 END — i.e. the year of the next August move-in.
Week of cycle (x-axis) derived DATEPART(ISO_WEEK, snapshot_date)
Freshness
Data as of (top-right pill) MAX(MarketReports.snapshot_date) Across all rows in data.json. Refreshed weekly when the export runs.

Known deviations from the dashboard spec

Documented gaps where the source can't perfectly match a.md/dashboard-spec.md.

  • Pipeline by stage, not 12/24/36mo delivery cohort. The source tracks pipeline as lease_up / under_construction / planned. There's no per-project delivery-date column with a clean marketKey join, so we expose the source's stage split instead. See g.docs/known-gaps.md §1.
  • 15 market_key values in MarketReports have no row in Markets. INNER-JOIN to Markets excludes those 15 from the dashboard. See known-gaps.md §3.
  • One Subtext-30 university unmapped to a marketKey. 29 of 30 currently flagged. See known-gaps.md §4.
  • Pre-leasing uses ISO week, not Jan-anchored week-of-cycle. ISO week gives a stable seasonal x-axis comparable across cycles. See known-gaps.md §2.
